    KFC, short for Kentucky Fried Chicken, is one of the world’s most iconic fast-food brands, famous for its crispy fried chicken made with the legendary “11 herbs and spices” recipe created by Colonel Harland Sanders in 1930. Over the decades, KFC has grown into a global chain with thousands of outlets across more than 150 countries, serving not just fried chicken but also burgers, wraps, rice bowls, and sides tailored to local tastes. Beyond food, KFC focuses on creating memorable dining experiences, supporting communities, and innovating while staying true to its original flavor and quality. Its red-and-white branding and friendly service have made it a household name for comfort food lovers worldwide.
